About Christian!

Hello World! My name is Christian Williams, and I am excited to start my year at Code School. I am 26 years old and stepping back into the great world of code. I am relearning code because I not only want to support myself and my family, but also to pursue personal projects. Technology is ever-changing and offers many job opportunities! I want to learn and grow as a coder so that I can make all my goals achievable, financial or creative!

I love writing and design. I went to Southwest Community College to pursue Graphic Design and Videogame Production and Design. In 2025, I was diagnosed with a disability that forced me to put my schooling on hold and focus on recovery. However, I am blessed and recovering well, focused on continuing my education and creative works! One of my major career goals is to obtain a steady-paying job for financial independence! Website and UI design, and/or front-end programming have been something I've been interested in. My second goal is to create and grow my own game studio. I have many worlds I want to share and need the means to build them. Programming is the blood; without it, all I have is a good story, but nothing else to bring the heart of the game to life.

Oh! Where do I begin to tell you about my favorite foods!? I am not a picky person! My family and I really love Asian and Mexican food. However, as long as it’s delicious, we have no problem eating it. Recently I’ve found a new Vietnamese place called Green Bamboo. They have a chicken pho that is so savory and filling- and the best fried rice you can get for miles. I also enjoy Southern soul food, and one of my guilty pleasures is ribs. I do not eat a lot of red meat, so when ribs are offered, I take the opportunity to eat my fill.
